How this is calculated

Money in is positive included value. Money out is the magnitude of negative included value. Transfers, Excluded and Hidden are not in these totals. Uncategorised is included and identified. Salary-cycle dates are explicitly confirmed from receipts; this example does not detect salaries. The confirmed date can be retained after the source is hidden or deleted.
